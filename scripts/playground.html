Copying a given sentence, the Aalto task.

Cursor moves and selection-deletes as a share of events. Geometric bins, not percents: real writers sit around 1.1–1.3% and the median corpus window at 0.39%, so a whole-percent scale put almost everything in bin 0. The slider picks the bin and shows the rate it means.

1.0 is the most realistic setting. Cooling measurably hurts: the discriminator catches 0.60 of samples at T=1.0, 0.66 at 0.9, 0.685 at 0.8.

Seeds the buffer, so the run becomes revise this into the target rather than type it from scratch. This is the way to watch draft→final revision: the mask can permit a long excursion but it cannot make that excursion a plausible earlier draft — only training can. Hand it one instead.

Events allowed off-path before repair is forced. This is the knob that limits how long a detour can run — not the excursion budget. At 10 a revision must resolve almost immediately; raising it lets the model stay off-target for longer, at the cost of the delete-everything failure returning.

The character-level typo allowance. Raising it alone does little: an excursion also has to be affordable (the remaining budget must still pay to undo it and finish) and fresh (within the staleness window), and those bind first.

On-path characters typed after a correction before excursions reopen. Stops the model oscillating.
